Who we are

Older women built this place. We still run it.

Our founding members imagined a building where women could grow older with dignity, affordable rent and friends down the hall. In 1997 the doors opened. They have stayed open ever since.

  • Run by members

    Every member has a vote. Our board is elected from our own neighbours.

  • Affordable for life

    Most homes are geared to income, so rent stays within reach as life changes.

  • Never far from company

    Tea on Tuesdays, a choir on Thursdays, and a garden that always needs a hand.
