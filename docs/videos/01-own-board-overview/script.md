# 01 · A website and a helping hand for OWN

## Welcome

Hello, and thank you for having us.

[pause 3s]

Some of us love the internet. Some of us would rather keep things simple, and that is perfectly fine. This platform is for everyone. It is a website, and it is also a helping hand.

Today we will show you six things. Each one is something you told us matters. For each one, we will say plainly what works today, and what comes next.

[pause 2s]

## Six things that matter

First, a website that is beautiful and easy to read. This is the home page we built for OWN. It is warm. It has big words, big buttons, and pictures of home. It works today, on a computer and on a phone.

[pause 2s]

Second, OWN runs it. Nobody else. Someone in your office signs in to a simple admin desk. Every purple tag on this screen marks a part of the website that the office can change by themselves. The words, the photos, the menu.

Today, the office can change the colours, and keep a library of photos. The office can also add and remove whole pages, and edit the list of your board of directors. So when you hold your yearly election, the new directors can appear on the website the same afternoon. No programmer needed.

[pause 2s]

Third, notices that nobody misses. The office writes a notice once. It shows on the front page of the website, and in the documents page for members. This works today. Sending a notice straight to your phone or email is a later step.

[pause 2s]

Fourth, a leaking tap should be easy to report. A member taps new request, types a few words, adds a photo if she likes, and sends it.

The office sees it at once, and taps receive, so the member knows someone is on it. When the work begins, the office taps start work, and when it is fixed, finish work. The member can follow the request from new, to received, to started, to done, with her photos beside it. All of this works today.

[pause 2s]

Fifth, bigger words. Any member can make the text bigger, all the way to four hundred percent, and still use the whole site. Everything stacks into one easy column, and nothing falls off the edge. This works on every screen today.

[pause 2s]

Sixth, your own face. A member can add her own profile picture, change it, or take it away. Neighbours see her face beside what she writes. This works today.

[pause 2s]

## Today and next

So, in short. All six things work today. The home page, the pages and the board list the office edits, the colours and the photo library, notices, repair requests with photos, bigger text, and profile pictures. Coming next is sending a notice straight to your phone or email.

[pause 2s]

## Our ask

We ask three small things. First, let two members try notices and maintenance requests. Second, tell us the first three pages you want on your website. Third, choose who in your office will be your admin.
