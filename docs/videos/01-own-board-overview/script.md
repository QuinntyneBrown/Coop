# 01 · A website and a helping hand for OWN

## Welcome

Hello, and thank you for having us.

[pause 3s]

Some of us love the internet. Some of us would rather keep things simple, and that is perfectly fine. This platform is for everyone. It is a website, and it is also a helping hand.

Today we will show you six things. Each one is something you told us matters. For each one, we will say plainly whether it works today, or whether it comes next.

[pause 2s]

## Six things that matter

First, a website that is beautiful and easy to read. This is the design we drew for OWN. It is warm. It has big words, big buttons, and pictures of home. It is not live yet, but this is the look we are building toward.

[pause 2s]

Second, OWN runs it. Nobody else. Someone in your office signs in to a simple admin desk. Every purple tag on this screen marks a part of the website that the office can change by themselves. The words, the photos, the menu.

Today, the office can already change the colours, and keep a library of photos. Coming next is adding and removing whole pages, and editing lists, like your board of directors. So when you hold your yearly election, the new directors can appear on the website the same afternoon. No programmer needed.

[pause 2s]

Third, notices that nobody misses. The office writes a notice once. It shows on the front page of the website, and in the documents page for members. This works today. Sending a notice straight to your phone or email is a later step.

[pause 2s]

Fourth, a leaking tap should be easy to report. A member taps new request, types a few words, and sends it.

The office sees it at once, and taps receive, so the member knows someone is on it. The member can follow the request from new, to received, to done. Reporting, receiving and following a request works today. Buttons for started and finished, and photos, come next.

[pause 2s]

Fifth, bigger words. Any member can make the text bigger, all the way to four hundred percent, and still use the whole site. Everything stacks into one easy column, and nothing falls off the edge. We design for this on every screen, and we will test it before anyone has to rely on it. This part is coming next.

[pause 2s]

Sixth, your own face. Members can add their own profile picture, so neighbours see who is writing. The screen is drawn, and a place to keep the pictures is ready. Joining them together is coming next.

[pause 2s]

## Today and next

So, in short. Working today are notices, maintenance requests, your colours, and a library of photos. Coming next are the new website design, pages and lists the office can edit, bigger text, and profile pictures.

[pause 2s]

## Our ask

We ask three small things. First, let two members try notices and maintenance requests. Second, tell us the first three pages you want on your website. Third, choose who in your office will be your admin.

[pause 1s]

Thank you. We would be glad to answer any question, however small.
