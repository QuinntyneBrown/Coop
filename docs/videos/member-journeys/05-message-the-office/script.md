# 05 · Message the office

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member reads and sends messages to the office.

[pause 2s]

Messages are a simple way to talk with the office, in your own time, without a phone call.

[pause 2s]

As a member, you see a simpler menu, with just five areas: Dashboard, Maintenance, Documents, Messages and My profile. Messages is the one we use today.

[pause 2s]

## Your conversations

On a computer, choose Messages in the menu on the left. On a phone, tap Messages at the bottom of the screen. You see your conversations with the office. Each shows its subject, the latest message, and the time.

[pause 2s]

A small label that says 1 new means there is a message you have not read yet.

[pause 2s]

## Read and reply

Tap a conversation to open it. Here it is Fan repair visit. The names at the top show who is taking part: Oren Reed, and Dolores Nakamura from the office. Each message shows who sent it, and when.

[pause 2s]

To reply, tap the box that says Write a message. Write your words, then tap the Send button beside it.

[pause 2s]

When you go back to the list, the new label is gone. You are all caught up.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Messages. Tap a conversation. Write your message and tap Send.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
