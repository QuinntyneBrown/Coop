# 01 · Submit a maintenance request

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member reports something that needs fixing at home.

[pause 2s]

A dripping tap, a light that flickers, a window that will not close. Telling the office should be easy.

[pause 2s]

When you sign in, you see a simpler menu with just five places: Dashboard, Maintenance, Documents, Messages and My profile. Everything you need is there, and nothing else gets in the way.

[pause 2s]

## Send a request

First, sign in to the co-op's site. Then choose Maintenance in the menu. On a phone, tap Requests at the bottom of the screen. Here you see every request you have sent, and where each one is.

[pause 2s]

To report something new, tap New request. A short form opens.

[pause 1s]

Give it a title, such as water under the kitchen sink. Then say what is wrong in your own words.

[pause 2s]

If you like, add a photo or two. Tap Add photos and pick them. A photo often says more than words. If you choose the wrong one, tap Remove.

[pause 2s]

When you are ready, tap Send request. The button says Sending while it goes.

[pause 2s]

## What happens next

Your request now shows on your list, marked New. You can open it at any time to see how it is going.

[pause 2s]

At the same moment, the office sees it, with your words and your photos. When they receive it, you know someone is on it.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Choose Maintenance. Tap New request. Say what is wrong, add a photo if you like, and send. That is all.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.
