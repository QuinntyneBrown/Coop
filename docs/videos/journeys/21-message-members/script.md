# 21 · Message members

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows the office how to send messages to members.

[pause 2s]

A quick question, a reminder, a friendly reply. Messages keep the office and members talking.

[pause 2s]

## Find or start a conversation

Start on the Messages page. Tap Messages in the menu. Your conversations are listed beside the menu. A number shows how many messages are unread.

[pause 2s]

Looking for one? Type a word in Search conversations. Clear the search to see them all again.

[pause 2s]

To start a new one, tap the add button beside Messages. The New Conversation box opens. Type who it is for in Recipient, then tap Start.

[pause 2s]

## Send a message

Tap a conversation to open it. You see who is in it, and the messages so far, each with its time.

[pause 2s]

Type your message in the box at the bottom, where it says Type a message. Then tap the send button. Your message joins the conversation, and the box clears, ready for the next one.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Messages. Search for a conversation, or start a new one. Type your message and tap send.
