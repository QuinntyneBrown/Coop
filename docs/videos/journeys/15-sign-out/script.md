# 15 · Sign out

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member signs out.

[pause 2s]

Signing out is a good habit, most of all on a shared computer, or in a library. It keeps your details private.

[pause 2s]

## Sign out

On a phone, tap Profile at the bottom of the screen. Your page, My profile, opens.

[pause 1s]

Move down the page, past your details. Under the Edit profile and Change password buttons is a button that says Sign out. Tap it.

[pause 2s]

On a computer, choose My profile in the menu on the left. The Sign out button is beside Change password.

[pause 2s]

## What happens next

You are signed out, and you see the sign in page. It says Welcome back.

[pause 2s]

Next time, type your username and password, and tap Sign in.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Profile. Tap Sign out. That is all.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
