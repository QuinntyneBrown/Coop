# 15 · Sign out

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member signs out.

[pause 2s]

Signing out is a good habit, most of all on a shared computer, or in a library. It keeps your details private.

[pause 2s]

## Sign out

Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen.

[pause 1s]

Move down the page, past your details and the Change Password button. At the bottom is a button with a red edge. It says Log Out. Tap it.

[pause 2s]

## What happens next

You are signed out, and you see the sign-in page. It says Welcome back.

[pause 2s]

Next time, type your username and password, and tap Sign In.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Profile. Tap Log Out. That is all.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
