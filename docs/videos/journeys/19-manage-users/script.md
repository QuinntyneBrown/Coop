# 19 · Manage users

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows the office how to look after the people who can sign in to the co-op website.

[pause 2s]

You can find someone, add someone new, change their details, or remove them.

[pause 2s]

## Find a user

Start on the Users page. Tap Users in the menu. Each row shows a username, a role, and a status, with buttons to edit or delete.

[pause 2s]

The list shows a few users at a time. Below it, you see how many there are. Tap a page number, or Next, to see the next page.

[pause 2s]

Looking for someone? Type part of their username in the search box. If nobody matches, the list is empty. Clear the search to see everyone again.

[pause 2s]

## Add, edit and delete

To add someone, tap Add User. Fill in the Username, choose a Role, and set a Password. Then tap Add user.

[pause 2s]

To change a user, tap the edit button on their row. A form opens with their details filled in. Make your change and tap Save changes.

[pause 2s]

To remove a user, tap the delete button on their row. You are asked if you are sure. Tap Delete user to remove them, or Keep the user if you change your mind.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Search or page through to find someone. Add User to add. Edit to change. Delete, then confirm, to remove.

Thank you for watching.
