# 05 · Message the office

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member reads and sends messages.

[pause 2s]

Messages are a simple way to talk with the office, and with your neighbours, without a phone call.

[pause 2s]

## Your conversations

Tap Messages at the bottom of the screen. You see your conversations. Each shows its subject, the latest message, and the date.

[pause 2s]

A small green number means there are messages you have not read yet.

[pause 2s]

## Read and reply

Tap a conversation to open it. The names at the top show who is taking part. Each message shows who sent it, and when.

[pause 2s]

To reply, tap the box that says Type a message. Write your words, then tap the green send button beside it.

[pause 2s]

When you go back to the list, the green number is gone. You are all caught up.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Messages. Tap a conversation. Type your message and send.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.
