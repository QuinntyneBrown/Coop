# 22 · Admin sign in and register

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how office staff get into the co-op's office screens.

[pause 2s]

You can sign in, create an account with an invitation, or find the forgot password page.

[pause 2s]

## Sign in

The sign in page says Welcome back. Type your username, then your password.

[pause 1s]

Tick Remember me if this is your own computer. Then select Sign In.

[pause 2s]

If the username or password is wrong, a message says Invalid username or password. Check your typing and try again.

[pause 2s]

## Create an account

New to the office screens? Select Sign up, under the Sign In button.

[pause 1s]

The Create account page asks for your invitation token. The office gives you this token. Then choose a username, and type your password twice.

[pause 2s]

Tick I agree to the Terms and Conditions, and select Create Account.

[pause 2s]

## Forgot password

If you forget your password, select Forgot password on the sign in page. The page asks for your username.

[pause 1s]

The reset does not send anything yet. For now, ask the person who looks after the system for help. Select Back to Sign In to return.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Sign in with your username and password. Use your invitation token to create an account. And ask for help if you forget your password.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
