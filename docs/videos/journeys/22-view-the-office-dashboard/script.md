# 22 · View the office dashboard

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows the dashboard, the first screen office staff see after signing in.

[pause 2s]

It gives a quick picture of the co-op today, all in one place.

[pause 2s]

## At a glance

At the top, a greeting says hello, with your name. Below it are four numbers.

[pause 1s]

Open Requests, Unread Messages, Documents, and Active Members. One look tells you how busy the day may be.

[pause 2s]

## Requests and notices

Next comes Recent Maintenance Requests. Each one shows its title, its date, and where it is: New, Received, Started, or Completed.

[pause 1s]

Select View all requests to see every request.

[pause 2s]

At the bottom, Recent Notices lists the latest notices, such as the autumn courtyard gathering.

[pause 2s]

## Quick actions

The Quick Actions save a few steps. New maintenance request, Create document, New message, and Send invitation each take you straight to that task.

[pause 2s]

The menu on the left reaches every other part of the office screens.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Check the numbers, look over recent requests and notices, and use a quick action to get started.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.
