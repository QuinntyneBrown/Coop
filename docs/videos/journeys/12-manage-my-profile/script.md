# 12 · Manage my profile

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member keeps her own details up to date.

[pause 2s]

Your profile holds your name, your email, your phone number and your picture. You can change them yourself, at any time.

[pause 2s]

## Your details

Sign in, then tap Profile at the bottom of the screen. On a computer, choose My profile in the menu. You see your name, your picture, your username, your email, your phone and your address.

[pause 2s]

To change something, tap Edit profile. Now you can type a new first name, last name, email or phone number.

[pause 1s]

You can also choose how new notices reach you. Tick By email, By text message, or both.

[pause 2s]

When you are happy, tap Save changes. If you change your mind, tap Cancel. A short message says Your profile is saved.

[pause 2s]

## Your picture

To add a picture, tap Choose a photo, or Choose another if you already have one. A JPEG, PNG or WebP photo, up to ten megabytes, is fine.

[pause 1s]

You see a preview first. Tap Save photo to keep it, or Cancel to leave things as they were.

[pause 2s]

The page says Your photo is saved. If you ever want it gone, tap Remove photo.

[pause 2s]

## Switch profile

Some members have more than one profile. If you do, they are all listed under My profiles. Tap the one you want.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Profile. Tap Edit profile to change your details, and Save changes. Tap Choose a photo to add a picture. That is all.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
