# 12 · Manage my profile

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member keeps her own details up to date.

[pause 2s]

Your profile holds your name, your email, your phone number and your picture. You can change them yourself, at any time.

[pause 2s]

## Your details

Tap Profile at the bottom of the screen. You see your name, your username, your email, your phone and your unit.

[pause 2s]

To change something, tap Edit. Now you can type a new first name, last name, email or phone number.

[pause 1s]

You can also choose how new notices reach you. Tick By email, By text message, or both.

[pause 2s]

When you are happy, tap Save Changes. If you change your mind, tap Cancel. A short message says Profile updated successfully.

[pause 2s]

## Your picture

To add a picture, tap the round picture at the top. Choose a photo. A JPEG, PNG or WebP photo, up to ten megabytes, is fine.

[pause 1s]

You see a preview first. Tap Save photo to keep it, or Cancel to leave things as they were.

[pause 2s]

The page says Your photo is saved. If you ever want it gone, tap Remove photo.

[pause 2s]

## Switch profile

Some members have more than one profile. If you do, tap the list under Switch Profile, and choose the one you want.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Profile. Tap Edit to change your details, and Save Changes. Tap the round picture to add a photo. That is all.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
