# 17 · Send a notice to members

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows the office how to send a notice to every member.

[pause 2s]

A courtyard gathering, a water shut-off, a change to the rules. A notice keeps everyone in the loop.

[pause 2s]

## Write the notice

Start on the Documents page. Here you see every document, with its type, its date, and whether it is a draft or published.

[pause 2s]

Tap New document. A short form opens.

[pause 1s]

Give the notice a title. Choose Notice as the type. Then write the message in Content, in plain words.

[pause 2s]

## Publish and send

Set the status to Published. A new choice appears: Also send to members by email and text. Tick it to send the notice out.

[pause 2s]

Only members who asked for new notices on their profile get one. Then tap Save document.

[pause 2s]

A message at the top of the page says the notice is published and sent to members who asked for notices.

[pause 2s]

If something goes wrong, the form stays open and says the notice was not sent. Press Save document to try again. Members still get it only once.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Open a new document and write a notice. Set it to Published, tick the send box, and tap Save document.

Thank you for watching.
