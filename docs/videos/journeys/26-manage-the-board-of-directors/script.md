# 26 · Manage the board of directors

## Welcome

Hello. This short video is for the co-op office. It shows how to keep the board of directors up to date on the website.

[pause 2s]

The home page shows the board under Meet the board. You choose who is there, and in what order.

[pause 2s]

## Add and edit

Start with Board, in the menu on the left. Here you see every director, in the order the website shows them. Changes are live as soon as you save.

[pause 2s]

To add someone, choose Add director. Type their name and their role on the board, such as Treasurer. If you have a portrait in the Assets library, paste its address. Without one, the website shows their initials. Then choose Save director.

[pause 2s]

To change a director, choose Edit beside their name. The same form opens. Make your change and choose Save director.

[pause 2s]

## Order and remove

To change the order, use Move up and Move down.

[pause 1s]

To take someone off the list, choose Remove. The office is asked to confirm. Choose Remove director, and they leave the home page straight away. You can add them again later.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Open Board. Add, edit, move, or remove. The website follows along.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
