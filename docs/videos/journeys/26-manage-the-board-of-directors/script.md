# 26 · Manage the board of directors

## Welcome

Hello. This short video is for the co-op office. It shows how to keep the board of directors up to date on the website.

[pause 2s]

The home page shows the board under Meet the board. There is one list of directors, and the home page reads only that list. You choose who is on it, and in what order.

[pause 2s]

## Add and edit

Choose Website in the menu on the left. Then, in the list of website parts, choose Board of directors. Here you see every director, in the order the home page shows them. This list does not wait for Publish. Changes are live as soon as you save.

[pause 2s]

To add someone, choose Add director. Type their name and their role on the board, such as Treasurer.

[pause 1s]

For a portrait, choose Choose photo, and pick one from the library, or upload a new one. Without a photo, the website shows their initials. Then choose Save director. A note confirms that they are now on the board list.

[pause 2s]

To change a director, choose Edit beside their name. The same form opens, filled in. Make your change and choose Save director.

[pause 2s]

## Order and remove

To change the order, use Move up and Move down. The home page follows the new order straight away.

[pause 1s]

To take someone off the list, choose Remove. You are asked to confirm. Choose Remove director, and they leave the home page straight away. You can add them again later.

[pause 2s]

The heading of that part of the home page is not edited here. You change it under Home.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Open Website, then Board of directors. Add, edit, move, or remove. The home page follows along.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
