# 20 · Manage documents

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows the office how to look after the co-op's documents.

[pause 2s]

Notices, by-laws and reports all live in one place, so members can find them.

[pause 2s]

## Find a document

Start on the Documents page. Tap Documents in the menu. Each document shows its title, its type, its date, and whether it is a draft or published.

[pause 2s]

To see one kind only, tap a tab along the top: All documents, Notices, Bylaws, or Reports.

[pause 2s]

Looking for something? Type a word in the Search by title box. If nothing matches, you see No documents found.

[pause 2s]

## Add and delete

To add a document, tap New Document. Give it a title, choose the type, and write the content. Choose Draft or Published, then tap Save document.

[pause 2s]

To remove a document, tap the delete button on it. You are asked if you are sure. Tap Delete document to remove it, or Cancel to keep it.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Use the tabs or search to find a document. New Document to add one. Delete, then confirm, to remove one.
