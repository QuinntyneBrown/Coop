# 04 · Sign in

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member signs in to the co-op's online desk.

[pause 2s]

Signing in keeps your requests, messages and profile private, just for you.

[pause 2s]

## Sign in

Start on the co-op's website. At the top, tap the button that says Member sign in. On a phone, it is inside the Menu.

[pause 2s]

The sign in page opens. It says Welcome back. Type your username in the first box, and your password in the second.

[pause 2s]

Then tap the big Sign in button. While it works, the button is greyed out, so you cannot tap it twice.

[pause 2s]

If something is not quite right, a message says We couldn't sign you in. Check your username and password, then try again. If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot password.

[pause 2s]

## You are in

Once you are in, you see your own home page. It greets you by name, and shows your recent requests and the latest notices.

[pause 2s]

The menu shows only what is yours: Dashboard, Maintenance, Documents, Messages and My profile. On a phone, these are at the bottom of the screen.

[pause 2s]

If you saved an old link to a member page on the website, it now brings you to this sign in page too. Sign in, and you are home.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Member sign in. Type your username and password. Tap Sign in. That is all.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.
