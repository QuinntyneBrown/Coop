# 04 · Sign in

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member signs in to the co-op website.

[pause 2s]

Signing in keeps your requests and messages private, just for you.

[pause 2s]

## Sign in

The sign in page says Welcome back. Type your username in the first box, and your password in the second.

[pause 2s]

Then tap the big green Sign In button. While it works, the button says Signing in.

[pause 2s]

If something is not quite right, a message says Invalid username or password. Check your typing and try again. If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot password.

[pause 2s]

## You are in

Once you are in, you see your own home page. It greets you by name, and shows your recent requests.

[pause 2s]

If you try to open a private page, such as Requests or Messages, before signing in, the website brings you to the sign in page first. That is to keep your things safe.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Type your username and password. Tap Sign In. That is all.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.
