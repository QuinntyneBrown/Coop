# 02 · Follow a maintenance request

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member follows a repair she has already asked for.

[pause 2s]

Once a request is sent, you will want to know how it is going. It is all in one place.

[pause 2s]

## Check the status

Tap Requests at the bottom of the screen, or Maintenance in the menu on a computer. Each request shows a coloured label. New means the office has it. Received means they have seen it. Started means the work has begun. Completed means it is fixed.

[pause 2s]

To see only some of them, tap one of the buttons at the top: All, New, Received, Started or Completed. Tap Completed, and only the finished repairs are shown.

[pause 2s]

## Open a request

Tap a request to open it. You see its title, its label, and what you wrote.

[pause 2s]

Under Comments, you and the office can leave notes for each other. Here Dolores, from the office, says they will look at the fan on Thursday morning.

[pause 2s]

To reply, type in the box that says Add a comment, then tap Send.

[pause 2s]

## Photos

Any photos you added show under Photos. You can add another at any time. Tap Add a photo and pick one from your phone.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Requests. Look at the label to see where things are. Tap a request to read comments, reply, and see photos.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
