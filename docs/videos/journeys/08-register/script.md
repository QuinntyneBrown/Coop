# 08 · Register

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how to create your member account.

[pause 2s]

The office sends you an invitation token. It is a code that says you belong. Keep it close by before you begin.

[pause 2s]

## Create your account

Open the page called Create account. You will see four boxes to fill in.

[pause 1s]

First, type your Invitation Token. Then choose a username. This is the name you will sign in with.

[pause 2s]

Next, choose a password, and type it again in Confirm Password. Then tick the box that says I agree to the Terms and Conditions.

[pause 2s]

When you are ready, tap Create Account. The button says Creating account while it works.

[pause 2s]

## If something is missing

If a box is left empty, a short note appears under it in red, such as username is required. If the two passwords are not the same, it says, passwords do not match. Fix the box, and try again.

[pause 2s]

If the token is not right, the page tells you. Call the office, and they will help.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Have your token ready. Fill in the four boxes, tick the box, and tap Create Account. Next, you will set up your profile.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
