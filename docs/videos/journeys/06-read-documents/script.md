# 06 · Read documents

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how a member finds and reads the co-op's documents.

[pause 2s]

By-Laws, reports and notices are all kept together, so you never have to search through paper.

[pause 2s]

## Find a document

Anyone can read them, without signing in. On the home page, scroll down to the bottom and tap Documents and bylaws. The Documents page lists everything, with the kind of document and its date.

[pause 2s]

To see one kind only, tap a button at the top. Tap By-Laws to see the rules we agree on together. Tap Reports to see reports, such as the September maintenance report. Tap All to see everything again.

[pause 2s]

## Read it

Tap a document to open it. It opens on top of the page, so you can read it in full.

[pause 2s]

If you would like a copy, tap Download. To close it, tap the cross in the corner.

[pause 2s]

If you are a member, the same documents are also on the members' site. After you sign in, choose Documents.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Tap Documents and bylaws. Choose a kind if you like. Tap a document to read it.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.
