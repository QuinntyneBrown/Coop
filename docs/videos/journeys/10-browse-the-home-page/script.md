# 10 · Browse the home page

## Welcome

Hello. This short video is a walk around the co-op's home page. Anyone can read it. You do not need to sign in.

[pause 2s]

The office chooses which parts the home page shows, and in what order. So your home page may look a little different from this one.

[pause 2s]

## At the top

At the very top is the co-op's name, and links to each part of the site. On the right is Member sign in.

[pause 2s]

Then the big welcome. A home of our own, in the heart of Toronto. Two buttons sit underneath. Book a visit, and How to apply.

[pause 2s]

Just below are a few numbers at a glance. One hundred and forty-two homes, opened in nineteen ninety-seven, and run by the women who live here.

[pause 2s]

## Down the page

Scroll down a little, and you find Who we are. It tells how older women built this place, and still run it.

[pause 2s]

Next is A look around. Photos of Tuesday tea, the choir, the rooftop garden and the lobby.

[pause 2s]

Then short blocks about our building and our community. A step-free entrance, two lifts, a choir, and neighbours who check in.

[pause 2s]

Then What's on. Each event shows its date, what it is, the time, and where. Everyone is welcome.

[pause 2s]

Next, Meet the board. These are the members elected by their neighbours, with their names and roles.

[pause 2s]

After that comes a word from a member, in her own words.

[pause 2s]

## At the bottom

Near the bottom is Visit or apply, with the office phone number. Below it is Visit or write, with the phone, the email, and links to every part of the site.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Read the welcome, then scroll down for who we are, what's on, and the board. The bottom of the page tells you how to reach us.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
