# 10 · Browse the home page

## Welcome

Hello. This short video is a walk around the co-op's home page. Anyone can read it. You do not need to sign in.

[pause 2s]

## At the top

At the very top is the co-op's name, and links to each part of the page. On the right is Member sign in.

[pause 2s]

Then the big welcome. A home of our own, in the heart of Toronto. Two buttons sit underneath. Book a visit, and How to apply.

[pause 2s]

## Down the page

Scroll down a little, and you find Who we are. It tells how older women built this place, and still run it.

[pause 2s]

Further down are short blocks about our building and our community. A step-free entrance, two lifts, a choir, and neighbours who check in.

[pause 2s]

Then What's on. Each event shows its date, what it is, the time, and where. Everyone is welcome.

[pause 2s]

Next, Meet the board. These are the members elected by their neighbours, with their names and roles.

[pause 2s]

## At the bottom

At the bottom is how to visit or write. The office phone number, the email, and links to every part of the site.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Read the welcome, scroll down for who we are, what's on, and the board. The bottom of the page tells you how to reach us.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
