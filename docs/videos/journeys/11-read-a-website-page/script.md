# 11 · Read a website page

## Welcome

Hello. This short video shows how to open one of the co-op's website pages. The office writes these pages, such as How to apply.

[pause 2s]

## Open a page

The links at the top of the home page lead to each part of the site. On a phone, tap Menu to see them. A list opens. Tap Close to put it away.

[pause 2s]

Tap the page you want. Here, we tap How to apply.

[pause 2s]

The page opens with its title at the top. In the menu, How to apply is now marked, so you know where you are.

[pause 2s]

Scroll down to read. This page tells you who can apply, and what happens next. If you need a hand with the form, there is a phone number to call.

[pause 2s]

To go back, tap Home, or any link at the top.

[pause 2s]

## If a page is missing

If a page has been taken down, you see a note that says, we can't find that page. Nothing is broken. Tap Go to the home page, or pick another link.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Open the menu, tap a page, and scroll to read. Tap Home to go back.

[pause 1s]

Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
