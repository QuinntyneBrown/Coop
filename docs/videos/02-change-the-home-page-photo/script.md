# 02 · Change the home page photo

## Welcome

Hello. This video is for the co-op office. It shows, step by step, how to change the big photo at the top of the public home page.

[pause 1s]

The same steps work for every other photo on the home page, so once you have done it once, you can do them all.

[pause 2s]

Here is the photo we are going to change. It sits beside the welcome, at the very top of the home page. Everyone who visits the website sees it first.

[pause 2s]

## Find the photo

Sign in to the office app, and select Website in the menu on the left.

[pause 1s]

The Website page lists every part of the public site, in the order visitors see it. First come the Header and Footer, which are on every page. Then the parts of the home page, from the top down.

[pause 2s]

The top of the home page is called the Hero. Its card shows a small copy of the current photo, and the big title beside it. Select Edit on the Hero card.

[pause 2s]

The Edit Hero form opens, with the words already filled in. Scroll down to the part called Photo.

[pause 1s]

Here you see the current photo, and its file name. Below it is the photo description. To change the photo, select Choose photo.

[pause 2s]

## Pick a photo

A window opens, called Choose a photo. It shows every photo in the Assets library. The photo in use now has a green Current label.

[pause 1s]

To use a photo that is already in the library, simply select it. Here we pick the rooftop garden.

[pause 2s]

The window closes, and the form shows your new photo. Notice that the photo description is now empty, and the cursor waits in it. That is on purpose. A new photo needs a new description.

[pause 2s]

## Upload a new photo

What if the photo you want is not in the library yet? Perhaps a member took a lovely picture at the summer party.

[pause 1s]

In the Choose a photo window, look below the photos for Upload a new photo. Select Choose File, and pick the photo from your computer.

[pause 1s]

It must be a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo, up to 10 megabytes. Most photos from a phone or camera are fine.

[pause 2s]

While it uploads, you see the word Uploading. When it finishes, the window closes and the new photo is in the form, ready to describe. It is also saved in the Assets library, so you can use it again later.

[pause 2s]

If something goes wrong, a red message tells you what to do. For example, we couldn't upload the photo, try again. Or, choose a photo smaller than 10 megabytes. Nothing on the website has changed yet.

[pause 2s]

## Describe and save

Now write the photo description. Some visitors use a screen reader, which reads the page aloud. The description is what they hear instead of seeing the photo.

[pause 1s]

Write one or two plain sentences about what the photo shows. For example. A woman in a sun hat waters the flowers in our rooftop garden, with the city behind her. Keep it under 250 characters.

[pause 2s]

When you are happy, select Save changes at the bottom of the form.

[pause 1s]

If the description is empty, the form does not save. A box at the top says something needs attention, and the description shows a red message. Write the description, and save again.

[pause 2s]

Once it saves, the form closes and a green message says, your changes are now on the website. The Hero card now shows the new photo.

[pause 2s]

## Check the website

Open the public website and look at the home page. The new photo is there, straight away. You do not need to publish anything else.

[pause 2s]

Changed your mind? Do the same steps again and pick the old photo. Nothing is ever lost from the library.

[pause 2s]

## Other photos

Every card on the Website page with a small photo has photos you can change the same way.

[pause 1s]

A look around holds the photo gallery. Each photo has its own Choose photo button, a caption, and a size. Our building and community has a photo beside each row. And A word from a member has the photo beside the quotation.

[pause 1s]

In each one, select Edit, then Choose photo, then describe it and save.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. Select Website. Select Edit on the Hero. Select Choose photo, and pick or upload a photo. Describe it in plain words. Then Save changes.

[pause 1s]
