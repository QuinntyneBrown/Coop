# 04 · Change the home page, block by block

## Welcome

Hello. This video is for the co-op office. It walks down the public home page, part by part, and shows where each part is changed in the office app.

[pause 1s]

Every part works the same way. Select Website. Select Edit on the part. Change the words, lists or photos. Then select Save changes. The home page changes straight away.

[pause 2s]

## The Website list

On the Website page, the cards under Home page are in the same order as the home page itself. Hero at the top. Visit or apply at the bottom.

[pause 1s]

Each card shows a short reminder of what is in it, such as its title, or how many photos it holds. A few things are common to every form. A box marked optional can be left empty, and then nothing shows. A box without that word must be filled in. And a part that holds a list has Move up, Move down, Remove, and an Add button at the end.

[pause 2s]

## Hero

First, the Hero. This is the big welcome at the top of the page.

[pause 1s]

Its form has a small heading above the title, the title itself, and a short introduction. The badge is the little label that sits on the photo, such as Welcoming members since 1997.

[pause 1s]

Then two buttons, the main button and the second button. Each has a label and an address, just like the menu links. And finally the photo, which the first video in this series shows step by step.

[pause 2s]

## At a glance

Just under the Hero are three big numbers. This part is called At a glance.

[pause 1s]

Each figure has two boxes. The figure itself, such as 142. And what it counts, such as homes, from bachelor to two-bedroom. Both must be filled in. Add a figure, or remove one, and the row of numbers follows. Remove them all, and the row goes away.

[pause 2s]

## Who we are

Next is Who we are. It has a small heading, a title, and an introduction that tells the co-op's story.

[pause 1s]

Below that are the value cards. Each card has a picture, a title and a short text. For the picture, choose one of three drawings. People together, a home with a heart, or a cup of tea.

[pause 2s]

## A look around

A look around is the photo gallery. It has a small heading, a title, and a list of photos.

[pause 1s]

Each photo has its own Choose photo button and description, a caption that shows on the photo, and a size. Wide photos stretch across, tall photos stand up, and small photos fill the gaps. Try a mix, and look at the result. Use Move up and Move down to change the order.

[pause 2s]

## Our building and community

Our building and community holds the rows with a photo on one side and a short checklist on the other.

[pause 1s]

Each row has a small heading, a title, the checklist, and a photo. Type the checklist with one item per line. Each line becomes one tick on the home page.

[pause 2s]

## What's on

What's on shows coming events. In this form you change the small heading, the title, and the introduction.

[pause 1s]

There is also the message visitors see when nothing is planned. For example, no notices right now, check back soon.

[pause 1s]

The event cards themselves are not typed here. They are the notices the office publishes from Documents. Publish a notice, and it shows here.

[pause 2s]

## Meet the board

Meet the board works the same way. Here you change only the small heading and the title.

[pause 1s]

The people, with their names, roles and portraits, come from the Board page in the menu on the left. The next video shows how to keep that list up to date.

[pause 2s]

## A word from a member

A word from a member is the big quotation, with a photo beside it.

[pause 1s]

Type the quotation without quote marks. The website adds them. Then who said it, such as a first name and the year she joined. Then choose the photo and describe it. Always ask the member before you put her words and her photo on the website.

[pause 2s]

## Visit or apply

Last is Visit or apply, near the bottom of the page. It has a small heading, a title, and an introduction.

[pause 1s]

Then the phone number, shown large so it is easy to read, the office hours, and one button with a label and an address.

[pause 2s]

## In short

So, in short. The cards on the Website page follow the home page from top to bottom. Select Edit on any part, change it, and save. Events come from Documents, and the people on the board come from Board.

[pause 1s]

The next video shows how to add your own pages, and keep the board up to date. Thank you for watching.

[pause 3s]
