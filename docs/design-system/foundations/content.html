Content

Plain, warm and respectful words for the people who run the co-op, in sentence case, with real verbs, specific numbers and no blame.

Overview

The admin is read by co-op staff and board members, many of them retired and some of them new to computers. They are volunteers and neighbours running a home for older women, not system operators. The admin talks to them the way a thoughtful colleague would: plainly, warmly, and with respect for their time and judgement. It says what something does, what happened and what to do next, in everyday words.

The newer admin screens (Pages, Board, Website) already write this way, and the microcopy library below is drawn mostly from them. Lift copy from there before writing new copy. The pattern page Content and tone shows these rules applied to whole flows.

Sentence case and punctuation

  • Sentence case everywhere: page titles, dialog titles, buttons, labels, tabs, table headings, sidebar sections and messages. Only the first word and names are capitalised: "Maintenance requests", "Add director", "Recent notices", "OWN Housing Co-op".
  • Roles as words: show "System administrator", "Board member" and "Member" to people; SystemAdministrator and BoardMember are internal names.
  • Curly quotes and apostrophes in product copy: ’ “ ”. Put a named thing in quotes when it sits inside a sentence: Remove “Our garden”?
  • No full stop on titles, labels, buttons or tabs; full stops on sentences in help, error and message text.
  • The ellipsis character (…), not three dots, for work in progress: "Saving…", "Signing in…", "Loading the board list…". Never in a placeholder or label.
  • Middle dot separates short facts on one line: "Meeting notes.pdf · 2 KB · 13 Oct 2026".
  • Contractions are fine and friendlier: "couldn't", "don't", "you're".
  • Spelling: Canadian, with British-style "colour", "neighbour" and "centre". Write "email", "by-law" (plural "by-laws"), "co-op", "sign in" (verb) and "sign-in" (noun).

  • Day before month, short month names, and the year in lists, because records last for years: "13 Oct 2026". Never all-numeric dates such as 10/13/26, which read differently in Canada, the US and Europe.
  • Digits for all numbers, including one to nine, because people scan them: "2 photos", "3 figures".
  • Plurals follow the number: "1 asset", "2 assets". "No" instead of "0" in sentences: "No pages yet".

Error messages

Formula: what did not happen, why if it helps, and what to do next, plus that nothing is lost whenever that is true. Say "we" for the admin's failures and "you" only for what the person can do. Never blame, never say "invalid", "error" or "failed" on their own, and never end a message without a way forward.

Empty states

Formula: a title that says what is not here yet (not "No data" or "Nothing found"), one or two sentences on what happens while it is empty and what will fill it, and one action that fills it. A search with no matches is different: say what was searched for and offer to clear it.

Usage

  • Call people what the co-op calls them: members, board members, directors, staff, visitors. Never "users" in copy people read, except on the Users screen, which manages accounts.
  • Say what happens next, especially when the website changes: "Changes are live as soon as you save."
  • Keep a message to one sentence that names the thing; keep a field error to one short instruction.
  • Write the accessible name as carefully as the visible text, and read it aloud.

Accessibility

  • Plain language helps everyone, and especially people reading in a second language or after a long day. Aim for short sentences and common words.
  • Link and button text stands alone (2.4.4): "View all maintenance requests", not "View all"; "Delete Meeting notes.pdf", not "Delete".
  • Counts in accessible names say what they count: "2 unread messages", not "2".
  • Roles and names are read aloud as words: "System administrator", not "SystemAdministrator"; "mira.fern" is fine because it is what she types.
  • Labels are not placeholders: "Search users" is a label; "Username or role" is a hint.

Drift from the implemented admin

  • Casing: older screens use Title Case ("Open Requests", "Recent Maintenance Requests", "New Maintenance Request", "Confirm Delete", "Save Changes", "Update Password", "Create Invitation"); the Pages, Board and Website screens use sentence case. The system uses sentence case everywhere.
  • Dates: the admin shows "Oct 13, 2026" and "Oct 13, 2026, 8:00 AM". The system writes "13 Oct 2026" and "13 Oct 2026, 8:00 am", matching the admin's own notice text ("Saturday 24 October from 2 to 4 pm").
  • Errors: "Failed to update password", "Registration failed. Please check your invitation token." and "Invalid username or password" give no next step; required-field errors say "Current password is required" rather than an instruction. The editors' style ("We couldn't save the page. Try again.", "Give the page a title") is the system's.
  • Success: "Password updated successfully", "Profile updated successfully" and "Theme saved successfully" do not name the thing; "“About us” is now on the website." does.
  • Confirmations: "Confirm Delete · Are you sure you want to delete this asset/document?" with a bare "Delete" button. The system names the thing in the title and the button.
  • Empty and no-results: the Assets no-results state repeats "No assets uploaded yet" although two assets exist; Users says "No users found" with "Showing 0-0 of 0 users"; Messages says "Select a conversation to start chatting" when there are none.
  • Punctuation: three dots in "Signing in...", "Search users...", "Search documents..." and "Search assets..." (also used as aria-label); a hyphen in "Showing 1-2 of 4 users". The system uses "…" and an en dash.
  • Roles: the Users table shows "SystemAdministrator" and "BoardMember"; the Roles screen already shows "System administrator" and "Board member".
  • Spelling: "email" and "e-mail" both appear, as do "By-Laws", "By-Law" and "bylaw", and "Customization" next to "colours". The system writes "email", "by-law" and Canadian spelling.
  • Settings theme tab: "Theme Customization · Choose the colours for the app" changes the public website, not the admin; the label should say so, for example "Website colours".
  • System terms reach people: "Aggregate" as a column heading on Roles & Privileges, "Invitation Token", "Scope: Global / Default".
