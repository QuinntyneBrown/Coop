Foundation

Elevation

A nearly flat system: three shadows for three jobs and four z-order layers. There is no dark theme and no stronger elevation level.

WCAG 2.2 AALight appearance onlyTokens: --shadow-*, --z-*

Overview

Coop is almost flat. Surfaces are separated by a hairline border and a change of background, not by shadow. Three shadows exist, each with one job, and four stacking layers keep overlays in order.

  • No dark theme. The product ships one light appearance, so elevation is never expressed through lighter surfaces, and no shadow has a dark variant.
  • No stronger levels. The mocks show no raised menu, popover, drawer or toast shadow beyond these three. Dialogs sit on a scrim, not on a shadow. Do not add levels 3, 4 and up.

Z-order

Four layers. Pick the layer; never write a raw z-index.

TokenValueLayer
--z-base0Normal flow
--z-sticky100Sidebar, top bar and bottom tab bar
--z-drawer200Mobile sidebar drawer
--z-modal1000Dialog overlay and its content

Dialogs sit above the drawer, which sits above the sticky bars, which sit above the page. A toast or menu is not a layer in the mocks.

Usage

  • Separate surfaces with a border and background first. Add a shadow only for the three jobs above.
  • Hover shadow appears with --duration-base; it is a response, not a resting state.
  • A selected item takes --shadow-selected plus an accent border. The ring is not a focus indicator.
  • Overlays use the layer tokens, not numbers: a new overlay chooses an existing layer.

Accessibility

  • Shadow never carries meaning alone. The selected state also has an accent border and aria-current.
  • Do not rely on a shadow to show a boundary; keep the hairline border, which survives forced colours.
  • A shadow must not reduce the contrast of text on or beside it; the shadows are faint so they do not.
  • Dialogs should trap focus and make the page behind inert while open. The mocks do not implement a focus trap; the design system requires one (see Dialog).
