Foundation

Shape

Six radii, from nearly square to fully round, and two border widths. Radius follows the element: controls are small, cards are large, pills are full.

WCAG 2.2 AALight appearance onlyTokens: --radius-*, --border-width-*

Overview

Shape is a six-step radius scale plus two border widths. Radius follows the size and role of the element: small marks are nearly square, form controls are gently rounded, cards are rounder, and pills and avatars are fully round. Borders are one hairline, with one thick marker.

Radius scale

--radius-xs4pxSmall marks
--radius-sm8pxInputs, buttons, alerts
--radius-md12pxNavigation items, dialogs, tiles
--radius-lg16pxCards
--radius-xl24pxMobile bottom sheets
--radius-full9999pxPills, avatars, dots

Border widths

Two widths. Hairline (1px) is every border: card edges, dividers, input edges. Thick (3px) is the marker on a selected row or step.

The 2px focus ring is --focus-ring-width, not a border width; see Accessibility.

The mocks' own variables also defined --radius-sm 8px, --radius-md 12px and --radius-lg 16px; the design system keeps those names and adds the extremes.

Usage

  • Use a token for every border-radius; do not write pixel values.
  • Nest radii inward: an element inside a card padded --space-4 uses a smaller radius than the card.
  • Use --radius-full for circles and pills rather than 50%, so a non-square element still gets a pill.
  • Borders are hairline unless marking selection. Do not invent other widths.

Accessibility

  • Control edges that identify an input must reach 3:1 against the background (WCAG 1.4.11); that is --color-border-strong, not the decorative --color-border-default.
  • Do not rely on a radius alone to show state. Selection uses colour, the thick marker and aria-current or aria-selected.
  • Under forced colours, borders become system colours, so keep borders (not only fills) on controls.
