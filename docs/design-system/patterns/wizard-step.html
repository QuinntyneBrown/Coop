Pattern · Flows

Wizard step

One purpose per screen: where you are, one heading, a few related fields or one choice, one primary action and a quiet Back. Admin onboarding, which a new staff or board member sees once after registering, is three of these in a row.

Overview

A wizard step is a full screen, not a dialog. It sits in the auth layout (a centred card under the co-op's logo mark) like sign-in and registration, so someone who has just made an account stays in the same calm place. There is no sidebar and no tab bar: the only ways on or back are the step's own actions.

  • Step indicator (p.steps) at the top of the card: dots for the eye, "Step 2 of 3" in words for everyone.
  • One heading as the screen's h1, and at most one line under it.
  • The step's content: nothing on the welcome step, a few related fields on the profile step, one radio group on the unit step.
  • Actions (.wizard__actions): one primary block button, a ghost Back under it, and "Skip for now" as a link under the card on steps that can be skipped.
  • No illustration. .wizard__art exists in the system, but the admin onboarding uses none; the logo mark is the only picture.

Keep a wizard to three or four steps. If it needs more, the person is better served by a normal screen they can return to.

Every step is the auth card with a .card.stack.wizard inside it. The welcome step has no form; the profile and unit steps have one each.

Responsive behaviour

  • The auth card is 26rem wide at most and full width below that, with the layout margin as gutter. At 320px (400% zoom) every field and button is full width and nothing scrolls sideways.
  • Actions stack at every width: the block primary, then the ghost Back centred under it. There is never a row of three equal buttons.
  • Fields stay in one column at every width, first name above last name, so the reading order never changes.
  • On a phone the welcome and unit steps fit on one screen; the profile step scrolls, and the primary action follows the last field.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Each step is one view with one h1. Fields are native inputs with <label for>; the unit choice is native radios in a fieldset with a legend (see radio group and the WAI-ARIA radio group pattern). The step indicator is plain text; its dots are aria-hidden.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabFields or the unit group, then the primary action, then Back, then Skip for now.
↑ / ↓In the unit group, moves the choice between units.
EnterIn a field, goes to the next step.

Focus

The steps change in place, so focus moves to the new step's h1 (with tabindex="-1") and a screen reader reads "Step 2 of 3" and the heading. Back returns to the previous step with its values kept.

Labelling

Optional fields say "(optional)" in the label; required fields carry no marker because they are the norm. The photo field's help text is joined with aria-describedby. The logo mark is decorative here, because the heading names the co-op.

Motion

The card enters like every auth screen (see motion choreography). The current dot widens over --duration-base when the step changes. Under reduced motion it changes at once; the words "Step 3 of 3" carry the information either way.

Touch

Each unit row is at least 24px tall with the whole label as its target; the primary button is 48px tall and full width. Back and Skip for now sit apart from the primary action so they are not pressed by mistake.

Content

  • The heading names the step in sentence case: "Welcome to OWN Housing Co-op", "Set up your profile", "Select your unit". No exclamation marks.
  • One line under the heading at most, and only when it reassures or explains: "You can change these later in your profile."
  • Buttons are verbs: Get started, Next, Go to dashboard. Never Continue, Submit or OK.
  • "Skip for now" is always the same words, always a link, always under the card.
  • Never say "click"; say what to choose. Never say "You’re all set" until the person has finished the last choice.

Do and don't

Sources

Drift from the implemented admin

  • Visual language. The admin draws onboarding in green with Outfit, an .onboarding-card.card, .btn .btn-primary/.btn-secondary with an 8px radius, light theme only, and no logo. This system uses the auth layout with the logo mark, .wizard, pill .btn--primary and .btn--ghost, and both themes.
  • Step indicator. .step-indicator .step-dot.active.current plus a separate "Step 1 of 3" div; the dots carry no hidden text. The system uses one p.steps with the dots aria-hidden and the words beside them.
  • No h1. Each step's heading is an h2 ("Welcome to Your Cooperative!", "Set Up Your Profile", "Select Your Unit") and nothing moves focus when the step changes. The system makes the heading the h1 and moves focus to it.
  • Labels not tied to fields. The profile form's .form-group labels have no for; the inputs are named only by aria-label, Phone is type="text", and nothing has autocomplete. The system uses <label for>, type="tel" and autocomplete, and marks optional fields.
  • Unit options. .unit-option is a div with a click handler and a .selected class: it cannot be reached by keyboard and is not announced as a choice (the mock capture marks it role="link"). The system uses a radio group.
  • Three equal buttons. The profile step's .btn-row holds Back, Skip and Next side by side. The system stacks one primary and a ghost Back, with "Skip for now" as a link under the card.
  • Copy. Title Case headings, "Get Started", exclamation marks, and "You're all set! Click below to go to your dashboard." shown before a unit is chosen. See content and tone.
  • Missing states. No validation, busy or failure state; Go to dashboard leaves even when saving the profile fails (mock note and source).
