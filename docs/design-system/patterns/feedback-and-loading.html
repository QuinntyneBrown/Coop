Pattern · Feedback

Feedback and loading

Every action in the Coop admin answers back in the place the person is looking: a spinner with words while a list loads, a busy button while something saves, a progress bar while a file uploads, and a status alert that says what changed. Nothing claims success before the server has said yes.

Overview

The admin's users are co-op staff and board members, many of them older and not in a hurry to trust a screen. Feedback has three jobs: say that something is happening, say what happened, and say what to do if it did not work.

  • Loading a list: a spinner with words ("Loading maintenance requests…") in a role="status" region, where the list will appear. For a list that usually takes more than a moment, a skeleton in the list's shape.
  • Saving: the pressed button turns busy (aria-busy="true") and keeps its width. The rest of the form turns read-only.
  • Uploading: a progress bar with a percentage, because a large PDF or photo can take a while.
  • Done: a success alert at the top of the list or page, in role="status", naming the thing that changed: "“About us” is now on the website."
  • Failed: a danger alert where the work was, in role="alert", that says nothing was lost and offers "Try again". Errors never disappear on their own.

The admin confirms on the page rather than in a floating toast: the message stays where the person is already looking and does not time out. A toast is for a short confirmation that has no natural place on the page; see notifications.

Responsive behaviour

  • Loading and status alerts take the full width of the content column at every size. Their text wraps; they never truncate.
  • A status alert sits directly under the page header, so it is in view after a dialog closes, on a phone as well as a desktop.
  • On phones the bottom tab bar is fixed; a toast, when one is used, sits above it, never over it.
  • A busy button keeps its width at every size, so the footer does not jump when it turns busy.
  • At 400% zoom the spinner stays beside its words and the progress percentage stays beside the bar, wrapping below only when there is no room.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Loading text and success alerts are live regions with role="status" (polite). Failures use role="alert" (assertive). Upload progress is a role="progressbar" with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, aria-valuenow and a label. A loading list has aria-busy="true"; its skeleton is aria-hidden. A busy button keeps its label as its name and adds aria-busy="true".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches "Try again" in a failure alert like any other button.
Enter / SpaceActivates "Try again". A busy button ignores presses until the request ends.

Focus

Feedback never steals focus, with one exception: a failed form submit moves focus to the error summary. After a dialog closes, focus returns to the button that opened it, and the success alert is announced without moving focus.

Labelling

Spinners are decorative (aria-hidden="true"); the words beside them are the message. Success and failure text names the thing ("“About us” is now on the website."), so it makes sense when read alone.

Motion

Spinners turn, skeletons shimmer and the progress bar slides. Under prefers-reduced-motion the shimmer stops, spinners stop after one turn and the bar jumps to each value; the words and percentages carry the meaning.

Touch

"Try again" is a full 44px button. Success alerts have no close button to hit by accident; they go when the person leaves the page or starts the next task.

Content

  • Loading says what is loading: "Loading maintenance requests…", "Loading the board list…". Not "Please wait".
  • Success names the thing and the result: "“About us” is now on the website.", "Meeting notes.pdf is in Assets.", "Your password is updated." Not "Saved successfully".
  • Failure says what did not happen, then what to do: "We couldn't save the page. Try again." "We couldn't load the pages. Check your connection and try again."
  • Partial success says both halves: "“Autumn courtyard gathering” is published, but it was not sent to members. Press Save to try again."
  • Busy buttons keep their label; the ring shows the wait. When text is shown, it uses the "-ing" form with an ellipsis: "Saving…", "Removing…".

Drift from the implemented admin

  • Silent spinners. Maintenance, Documents and Messages load with .loading holding a Material Icons sync glyph with .spin, no words and no live region; the icon's ligature text "sync" may even be read out. The system uses .loading with .spinner, words, and role="status", as Pages, Board and Website already do.
  • One spinner per feature. .pages-spinner, .board-spinner and .website-spinner are three copies of one thing; the system has one .spinner with --sm and --lg.
  • One alert per feature. .pages-alert, .board-alert, .website-alert and .notice-alert (each with --success and --danger) plus the generic .alert .alert-success .alert-danger map to .alert with --success, --danger, --info and --warning.
  • Status without a live region. "Theme saved successfully" and "Password updated successfully" are plain .alert.alert-success with no role, so screen readers are not told. The system gives every success alert role="status" and words that name the change.
  • Success after failure. Settings (Theme) and both profile screens show their success message even when the save fails; change password keeps its required-field errors on screen after a successful save. The system shows success only after the server confirms.
  • Upload progress. The asset upload's busy state shows .progress-bar .progress-fill at 0% with no label and the Upload button still enabled. The system uses a labelled role="progressbar" with a value and disables the footer.
  • Busy cues. Document create shows "Saving…" without aria-busy; change password only disables the button. The system uses .btn[aria-busy="true"].
  • Visual language. Green Outfit alerts with Material Icons become the system's token-coloured alerts with stroke icons, in light and dark themes.
