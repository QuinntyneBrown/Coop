Pattern · Overlays

Dialogs and overlays

The Coop admin does most of its adding and editing in dialogs over the list they change: add a user, invite a member, log a maintenance request, upload a file, edit a page. Removing anything asks first, in a small dialog that starts on the safe button. On phones every dialog rises from the bottom as a sheet.

Overview

A dialog keeps the list in view behind it, so the person never loses their place. Every admin dialog is built from the same dialog parts: a header with the title and a close button, a body, and a footer with the way out first and the action last.

  • Form dialog: a few fields that add to or change the list behind it. Add a user, invite a member, log a maintenance request, new document, upload a file, new page, add a director, edit a website block.
  • Confirmation dialog: before anything is removed. It names the thing, says what happens next, and starts on the safe button. Remove a page, delete a user, delete a document or file, remove a director.
  • Result dialog: shows something the person needs to copy or keep before going on. The invitation code after an invitation is created.
  • Viewer dialog: reads one item without leaving the list. A document, a file's details.
  • Stacked dialog: a second dialog that picks something for the first. The photo picker over the website block editor.

When to use

NeedUseAdmin example

A long form, or something people link to or come back toA page, not a dialogChange password, profile, settings
Tell someone something happenedA status alert on the page, never a dialogSee feedback and loading

Stacking

  • One dialog at a time, with two exceptions: a picker that fills a field in the dialog below (the photo picker), and a confirmation for removing the item the dialog below shows (delete a file from its details). Never more than two deep.
  • The lower dialog stays open and inert behind its own backdrop. Closing the upper one returns focus to the button in the lower one that opened it.
  • Toasts (--z-toast) sit above dialogs (--z-modal) and the backdrop (--z-overlay); menus (--z-dropdown) sit below the backdrop.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 640px a dialog is a bottom sheet: full width, rounded top corners (--radius-xl), rising from the bottom over --duration-slow. The footer is sticky so the action is always in reach, and the body scrolls on its own.
  • From 640px it is centred, up to 32rem wide, with --radius-lg corners, and scales in over --duration-slow.
  • The panel's height is capped at the window minus 48px, so the backdrop always shows and nothing is cut off at 400% zoom.
  • The footer wraps; the primary or danger button stays last, landing on its own line on a narrow phone.
  • Two short fields may sit side by side inside a dialog from 768px of window width (a link's label and address); below that they stack.
  • A stacked dialog on a phone sits fully over the lower sheet; closing it shows the lower sheet again where it was.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Form, result and viewer dialogs follow the WAI-ARIA APG Dialog (Modal) pattern: role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby pointing at the title and aria-describedby at the purpose line. Confirmations follow the Alert Dialog pattern (role="alertdialog"). The native <dialog> element opened with showModal() is the preferred build: it makes the page behind inert and handles Esc. With a div, set inert on everything outside the dialog yourself.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the dialog's controls only; focus does not leave it while it is open.
EscCloses the dialog as if "Cancel" was pressed. Does nothing while the dialog is busy. In a stacked dialog, closes only the top one.
EnterIn a form dialog's single-line field, submits. On a focused button, presses it.
SpacePresses the focused button; picks a photo in the photo picker.

Focus

On open, focus moves to the first field of a form dialog, the safe button of a confirmation, or the title of a viewer. On close, focus returns to the button that opened the dialog. When that button is gone (the item was removed), focus goes to the list's heading and the status alert says what happened.

Labelling

Every dialog has a visible title that names its job and the thing ("Remove “Our garden”?"). The close button is an icon button named "Close". The danger icon is decorative; the title and the button label carry the meaning.

Motion

The backdrop fades in over --duration-base; the sheet slides up, or the centred panel scales in, over --duration-slow. Under prefers-reduced-motion both appear at once.

Touch

Footer buttons are 44px tall with 12px between them; the close button is a 44px icon button. On phones the footer is sticky at the bottom of the sheet, where thumbs reach. Tapping the backdrop closes a form dialog only when nothing has been typed; a confirmation never closes from the backdrop.

Content

  • Titles say the job in sentence case: "Add a user", "Invite a member", "New maintenance request", "Upload a file", "Choose a photo".
  • A purpose line under the title says what happens after saving: "The page appears in the website menu as soon as you save."
  • Confirmations ask a question that names the thing ("Delete the user tavi.moss?"), say what happens and what stays, and use outcome buttons: "Keep the user" and "Delete user".
  • The action button names the result: "Create invitation", "Send request", "Upload file", "Save page", "Remove page". "Cancel" is the way out of a form; "Done" closes a result.
  • No "Are you sure?" The question in the title already asks.

Drift from the implemented admin

  • Five dialog families. .modal-overlay .modal-content .modal-actions, .form-dialog (documents), .page-dialog, .director-dialog, .content-dialog and .photo-dialog each style the same thing. The system has one: .backdrop, .dialog, .dialog__panel, __header, __title, __body, __footer, __icon.
  • Dialogs that are not dialogs. The .modal-overlay dialogs (add user, edit user, delete user, maintenance request, invitation, invitation created, asset upload, details and delete, document viewer) have no role, no aria-modal and no label, and nothing keeps focus inside. The document dialogs add role="dialog", aria-modal and aria-labelledby; the hand-written pages, board and website dialogs use a native <dialog>. The system follows the native-dialog model.
  • No close button, or an unnamed one. Most .modal-content dialogs have no close button; the document viewer's .icon-btn shows the Material Icons ligature "close" with no aria-label. The system gives every form, result and viewer dialog a "Close" icon button.
  • Confirmations that do not name the thing. "Confirm Delete" with "Are you sure you want to delete this document?" or "this asset", and a bare "Delete". Only page remove ("Remove “Our garden”?", "Remove page", focus on Cancel) names it; the system follows it everywhere and starts every confirmation on the safe button.
  • Stacking. The asset delete confirmation hides the details dialog instead of stacking over it, and Cancel brings it back. The photo picker stacks correctly over the block editor with its own backdrop. The system allows exactly these two kinds of stacking and returns focus to the lower dialog.
  • Result dialog. The invitation code sits in a p.token-display with no label, "Copy" gives no feedback, and the way out is a primary "Close". The system labels the code, announces "Copied", and closes with "Done".
  • Viewer actions. The asset details' Download button does nothing. Recorded; the system does not show actions that do not work.
  • Phones. The implemented dialogs stay centred cards at every width; the system turns them into bottom sheets under 640px with a sticky footer.
  • Visual language. Green 8px-radius buttons and 12 to 16px card corners in Outfit become the system's raised surface (--color-bg-surface-raised, --shadow-4), --radius-lg panels and pill buttons, in light and dark.
