Pattern · Data display

Lists and tables

The admin shows co-op records in three shapes: a table for the people who can sign in, a list for documents and maintenance requests, and a short list on the dashboard. A search field or a row of filters sits above each one, and long tables are split into pages.

Overview

Staff and board members use these screens to find one record and act on it: a member who needs a new role, a notice to take down, a leaking tap to follow up. Every collection follows the same order from top to bottom, so a person who has learned one screen has learned them all:

  1. Page header: the screen's title on the left and its one primary action on the right ("Add user", "New document", "New request").
  2. Find: a labelled search field, a row of pill tabs that filter by type or status, or both.
  3. The collection: a table in a flush card (Users), or a list (Documents, Maintenance, the dashboard's recent requests). Each row carries a status badge and at most two icon buttons.
  4. Pagination, only where the collection is split into pages (Users), with a summary of what is showing.

When to use

UseWhenAdmin example
TableSeveral short facts per record that people compare down a column, and actions on each row.Users: username, role, status, actions
ListA title, one or two lines of detail and a status. Reads on a phone without scrolling sideways.Documents; Maintenance requests; Recent maintenance requests on the dashboard
SearchThe collection can grow past what fits on one screen and people know the name they want.Search users; Search documents
Pill tabs as filtersA small, fixed set of types or statuses, each worth seeing on its own.Documents: All documents, Notices, Bylaws, Reports. Maintenance: All, New, Received, Started, Completed
PaginationA table that is longer than a screen. Lists in the admin stay whole.Users
  • Newest first. Maintenance requests are ordered by the date they were sent, newest at the top.
  • The selected tab and the results agree. If a search runs across every type, no single tab stays selected while it shows.
  • Say what the search covers. Users are found by username; documents by title. The placeholder says so.
  • No bulk selection, sorting or infinite scroll. None of the admin collections has them.

Responsive behaviour

  • Page header: the primary action sits beside the title from 640px and wraps under it below that.
  • Search is full width on phones and capped at 24rem above that, so the text stays close to its label.
  • Pill tabs wrap onto a second line rather than scrolling, so no filter is hidden off-screen.
  • Lists keep one row per record at every width. The title and meta wrap inside .list__body; the badge and icon button keep their size. At 320px (400% zoom) nothing scrolls sideways.
  • The Users table scrolls sideways inside its .table-wrap when it does not fit, never the page. WCAG 1.4.10 allows this for data tables. Keep it to four columns so at most one scrolls out of view.
  • Pagination: the summary and the page links wrap onto separate lines on narrow screens; every link stays at least 44px.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Lists are <ul>; each row has one link, its title. The Users table is a native <table> with a visually hidden <caption> and scope="col" headers. Filters follow the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern with manual activation (see tabs). Pagination is a <nav> with its own label and aria-current="page". The search field is a native type="search" input with a visible label.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through the primary action, the search, the selected filter tab, each row's title link and row actions, then the page links.
← / →Moves between filter tabs; Enter or Space applies the one in focus.
EnterOpens the document or request, follows a page link, or runs a row action.
EscIn the search field, clears the text.

Focus

The shared ring draws around links, tabs and icon buttons. Choosing a filter tab or a page link leaves focus where it is; the status summary announces the new range. When a delete dialog closes, focus returns to the list, never to the top of the page (see dialogs and overlays).

Labelling

Every row action is named for its row: "Edit mira.fern", "Delete Autumn courtyard gathering". Avatars and type icons are aria-hidden because the name or type is written beside them. The pagination summary is a polite status, so searching or changing page announces "Showing 1 to 2 of 4 users".

Motion

Row hover and tab selection tint over --duration-fast; the loading spinner turns. Under reduced motion the tints are instant and the spinner stops after one turn; the words "Loading documents…" still say what is happening.

Touch

Filter tabs and page links are at least 44px tall. Icon buttons are 40px square with a 4px gap, above the 24px minimum, and sit at the end of the row away from the title link. Nothing depends on hover.

Content

  • Column headers and tabs are short nouns in sentence case: "Username", "Role", "Status"; "All documents", "Notices", "Bylaws", "Reports".
  • Role names read as words: "System administrator", "Board member", "Member", not the stored values SystemAdministrator and BoardMember.
  • Statuses are one word and always the same word for the same state: "Active"; "Published", "Draft"; "New", "Received", "Started", "Completed".
  • Dates as the admin writes them: "Oct 13, 2026"; with a time, "Oct 13, 2026, 8:00 AM".
  • The summary uses "to", not a dash: "Showing 1 to 2 of 4 users". A screen reader can read "1-2" as "one minus two".
  • Empty and no results say different things: "No requests yet" with the screen's action; "No users match “zed”" with "Clear search". See content and tone.
  • Search placeholders are hints, not labels: the label says "Search users"; the placeholder says what to type, "Part of a username".

Do and don't

Sources

Extracted from these admin mocks:

  • Users table, search and pagination: admin-users/default, page-2, empty, no-results
  • Documents list and filters: admin-documents/default, notices, bylaws, reports, loading, empty, no-results
  • Maintenance requests: admin-maintenance/default, new, received, started, done, loading, empty, no-results

Drift from the implemented admin

  • Visual language. The admin draws these screens in green with Outfit and Material Icons, light theme only: .btn .btn-primary, .card with a 12 to 16px radius, .badge .badge-success/-warning/-info/-gray. This system uses the violet accent, rounded system fonts, inline stroke SVG icons, .btn--primary, .badge--neutral for Received, and both themes.
  • Search has no visible label. .search-input on Users and Documents is a plain type="text" whose only name is aria-label copied from the placeholder ("Search users..."). The system uses .search with a visible <label>, type="search" and a clear button.
  • Search ignores the selected tab. On Documents, typing in the search shows matches of every type while the selected filter tab stays highlighted, so the tab and the results disagree. User search matches usernames only, though nothing on screen says so.
  • Filters are not exposed as a group. .filter-tabs .filter-btn (Documents) and .filters .filter-btn (Maintenance) are plain buttons whose only selected cue is the .active class. The system uses .tabs.tabs--pill with role="tab" and aria-selected. The Documents tab says "By-Laws" and the type says "By-Law"; the system writes "Bylaws" and "Bylaw".
  • Row actions are named by ligature text. .icon-btn buttons hold only a Material Icons span, so their names are "edit" and "delete" on every row. The system uses .btn.btn--ghost.btn--icon with an aria-label naming the row.
  • Cards are fake links with nested buttons. .document-card and .request-card are div role="link" tabindex="0"; the document card also holds the delete .icon-btn. The system makes the title a real <a> inside a .list__item.
  • Empty and no results are the same. Users shows a table row "No users found" with "Showing 0-0 of 0 users" in both empty and no-results; Documents and Maintenance show one .empty-state text for both. The system uses .empty with different copy and actions for each.
  • Loading has no words. .loading holds only a spinning Material Icons glyph (.spin). The system's .loading pairs .spinner with text in a role="status".
  • Pagination. .pagination .page-controls uses « and » characters as the only names of Previous and Next, marks the current page with .active only, and pages two users at a time. The system uses nav.pagination with aria-current="page", named Previous and Next links, a status summary that says "1 to 2", and leaves page size to the screen.
  • Role names and avatars. The table prints stored role values (SystemAdministrator, BoardMember) and gives each .user-avatar a random inline background colour. The system writes role names as words and uses .avatar on its accent tokens.
  • Headings. Collections are titled with <h3> under the page <h1> ("All Requests"), skipping a level, in Title Case. The system uses the next level down, in sentence case.
