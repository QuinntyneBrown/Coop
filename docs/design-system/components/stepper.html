Stepper

A row of dots with a step label that shows progress through the short, linear onboarding flow.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableDecorative progress indicator

Overview

The stepper shows progress through the onboarding flow as a row of small dots with a text label beside it ("Step 2 of 4"). Dots are a progress indicator, not controls: people move with the Back, Next and Skip buttons on the step.

  • Use it for a short, linear flow of three to four steps. It does not allow jumping to a step.
  • The visible "Step n of N" text is the source of truth; the dots repeat it visually.

Anatomy

  1. Indicator (.step-indicator)Centred row of dots, --space-3 gap, --space-8 space below.
  2. Dot (.step-dot)10px circle in --color-border-strong (upcoming).
  3. Completed dotAccent at 50% opacity.
  4. Current dotAccent, scaled to 1.2.
  5. Label"Step 3 of 4", --text-body-sm, muted.

Variants

One variant. The admin onboarding has three steps and the public onboarding four; the markup and styling are the same.

Step 3 of 4

Four steps with the third current; the first two are completed.

Sizes

Dots have one size: 10px, scaled to 1.2 when current.

States

Each dot is in one of three states, shown in order of progress.

UpcomingCurrentCompleted

Whole flow

Step 1 of 4

Step 2 of 4

Step 4 of 4

First, second and last step of the four-step public flow.
  • Current is aria-current="step"; the stylesheet also styles the mocks' .active class.
  • The admin flow marks every reached dot .active and adds a .current ring (3px, 20% green) to the current one. The shared stylesheet does not implement that ring; the system uses the completed and current dots instead (see Drift).
  • Dots animate colour and scale over --duration-slow (300ms).
  • No error, skipped or disabled dot is implemented.

Responsive behaviour

  • No @media rule targets the stepper. The row is centred and four dots plus label fit at 320px.
  • The step actions below it (Back, Next, Skip) are laid out by the onboarding page; the page reserves 80px of bottom padding so they clear the bottom tab bar.

Theming

The product ships one light appearance; there are no theme variants.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

No ARIA widget. The dots are decorative (aria-hidden="true") and the visible text "Step 3 of 4" carries the meaning. If the label should be announced when the step changes, put it in a polite live region (role="status"); the mocks do neither. If the dots are ever exposed, use an <ol> with aria-current="step" on the current item (WAI-ARIA aria-current value step).

Keyboard

The dots are not focusable and have no keyboard behaviour. Navigation is by the Back, Next and Skip buttons, which follow normal Tab and Enter behaviour. Moving to a new step should move focus to the step heading (requirement; not implemented).

Focus

Nothing in the stepper takes focus.

Labelling

The text "Step n of N" is the label and must always be present, because colour and scale alone do not convey progress (WCAG 1.4.1). The current dot also scales by 20%, so it is not colour alone, but the completed dot is faint.

Contrast

Upcoming dot on surface (non-text)
Current dot on surface (non-text)
Label text on surface

The completed dot is the accent at 50% opacity on white, about 2.2:1. That falls under the 3:1 non-text minimum, so it is only acceptable because the text label repeats the information; do not use dots without the label.

Motion

Colour and scale transition over --duration-slow. The tokens reduce the duration to near zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Not interactive; no touch target.

Content

  • Label format: "Step {n} of {total}".
  • The step heading below names the step ("Welcome to Your Cooperative!"); the stepper does not repeat it.
  • Keep the total accurate and the same on every step.

Drift

Where the mocks differ from each other or fail WCAG 2.2 AA, the design system resolves it as follows.

  • Admin marks every reached dot .active and rings the current one with .current; public marks earlier dots .completed and the current .active. The system uses the public form and aria-current="step".
  • Admin dots are a flat row of <div> and public dots sit in a .step-dots wrapper with a .step-label; the system uses an <ol> with the text label beside it.
  • Upcoming dot is the light divider colour (1.2:1) in the mocks; the system uses --color-border-strong (3:1).
  • The label is 13px in the mocks; the system uses 14px (--text-body-sm).
  • No live region or focus move on step change; both are requirements.
