Component · Identity

Avatar

A circle that stands for one person: their initials on a soft violet circle, or their own photo over the initials. Staff meet it in the sidebar footer, the Users table, conversations, profiles and the Board.

Overview

Use an avatar beside a person's name wherever a screen is about people: who is signed in, who a user account belongs to, who a conversation is with, whose profile is open, who sits on the board. It helps people find a row by eye; the name beside it does the identifying.

Every avatar uses the same colours: --color-fg-accent initials on --color-accent-subtle. Colour never tells people apart; the initials and the name do. When a person has a photo, the photo covers the initials, and the initials show again while it loads, if it fails and when it is removed.

An avatar is for people only. A conversation, a document or an asset is not a person; give it an icon in a list row instead. To change someone's photo, use the photo picker.

Anatomy

  1. Circle.avatar: a --radius-full grid box sized by --avatar-size, filled with --color-accent-subtle.
  2. InitialsOne or two capital letters in --text-label and --color-fg-accent, centred.
  3. Photo (optional).avatar__img covers the circle, cropped to fill it, and fades in. The initials stay underneath.

Variants

Two variants: initials, and photo over initials. Both use the same markup; the photo is one extra element.

Responsive behaviour

  • An avatar keeps its size at every width; it never stretches or shrinks with the screen. It is flex: none, so a long name beside it wraps instead of squashing the circle.
  • Sizes are in rem, so the circle and its initials grow together when people zoom or enlarge text.
  • On phones, Board cards keep the 56px avatar beside the name and move the actions below.

Theming

The avatar reads two role tokens: --color-accent-subtle for the circle and --color-fg-accent for the initials. In the dark theme the circle becomes a deep violet and the initials a light violet; the pair passes 4.5:1 in both. To re-theme avatars, change those two roles; never give a person their own colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Next to a visible name the avatar is decoration: aria-hidden="true" on the <span> and alt="" on its photo, so a screen reader reads "mira.fern" once, not "M F mira.fern". Where an avatar stands alone, give it role="img" and aria-label with the person's name, for example "Mira Fern".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabPasses over the avatar; it is never a focus stop in the admin.

Focus

None. If an avatar ever opens something, it must be the face of a real <button> or link with the shared focus ring and a name, not a clickable span.

Labelling

The name beside the avatar is the label. A photo inside a labelled avatar has alt=""; describing the photo would repeat the name.

Contrast

Initials on circle
Initials on circle

The circle itself is decoration next to text, so 1.4.11 does not apply to its edge.

Motion

A photo fades in over --duration-base; the uploading ring turns on --duration-loop. Both are zeroed or stopped after one pass under prefers-reduced-motion.

Touch

Not a target in the admin.

Content

  • Two capital letters from the person's name: first name and last name. Mira Fern is "MF"; June Okonkwo-Baptiste is "JO".
  • When only a username is known, use its first letter: mira.fern is "M".
  • Never emoji, symbols or a generic person icon in place of initials, and never the first letter of something that is not a person.
  • Photos are of the person, chosen by them or by staff with their permission.

Drift from the implemented admin

  • The Users table's .user-avatar gets an inline background picked from six hard-coded colours (green, blue, amber, red, violet, pink) by the username's first character, with white letters. White on the amber is about 2.2:1 and on the blue about 3.7:1, both under 4.5:1. The system uses one pair, --color-fg-accent on --color-accent-subtle, for everyone.
  • The sidebar's .user-avatar is a fixed green with white 14px letters (about 4.2:1, under 4.5:1 for text that size) and 36px; the system uses .avatar--sm (32px).
  • The profile's .avatar-circle is 100px, green, with 36px initials; the system's largest avatar is .avatar--xl (64px).
  • Initials differ by screen: one letter of the username ("M") in the sidebar and Users table, two letters of the name ("MF") in Profiles. The system uses two letters of the name whenever the name is known.
  • Messages' .conv-avatar and .chat-avatar show the subject's first letter ("C"); the system shows the other person's initials, and an avatar only ever stands for a person.
  • Board's .director-card__photo already matches the system: 56px, initials on a soft accent circle, photos with alt="" beside the name.
  • None of the implemented avatars hide their letters from screen readers; the system marks them aria-hidden="true" next to a name.
  • Global: the implemented admin uses Outfit, Material Icons and a light theme only.
