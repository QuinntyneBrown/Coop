Placement note

One plain sentence under a section heading that says where the section appears on the public page.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatic text

Overview

The placement note is one plain sentence under a section's heading in the page editor that says where the section appears on the public page: "Appears first on the home page, under the menu." It replaces guessing from a block's name. Every section has one, including the menu ("Appears at the top of every page") and a page's body blocks ("Appears in the page body, in this order").

  • Use it once per section form, right under the section heading and before the fields.
  • Do not use it for help about a field; that is field help.

Anatomy

  1. Pin iconA 16px place marker, aria-hidden.
  2. SentenceBody-small muted text on a subtle fill, in a <p>.

Variants

One variant. The sentences are fixed per section kind.

Appears first on the home page, under the menu.

Appears at the bottom of every page.

Appears in the page body, in this order.

Home, footer and a page block.

Sizes

One size: --text-body-sm.

States

The note is static text and has no interactive states.

Both themes

Appears under the numbers, with three value cards.

Appears under the numbers, with three value cards.

Responsive behaviour

  • The sentence wraps beside the icon; at 320px it takes two or three lines and never scrolls.

Theming

--color-bg-subtle behind --color-fg-muted, both themes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A paragraph. It is not an aria-describedby target of the form: it reads in order after the heading, where a screen-reader user meets it anyway.

Keyboard

Not focusable.

Focus

None.

Labelling

The icon is decorative. The sentence names the neighbouring sections in quotes, as the section list names them.

Contrast

Sentence on subtle fill

Motion

None.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Start with "Appears", then the place in words a visitor would use: "first on the home page, under the menu", "under “Who we are”, as a grid of photos with captions", "last on the home page, above the footer".
  • Name neighbours by their section names in quotes; never by number, since numbers change when sections move.
  • One sentence, with a full stop.
