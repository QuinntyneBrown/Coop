Publish bar

One sentence that says whether the website has unpublished changes, with Preview site, Publish and Discard changes. It is the unpublished-changes banner of the Website area.

Overview

The publish bar sits at the top of every Website screen, under the page header. It says in one sentence whether the site has unpublished changes, and holds the three actions on the draft: Preview site (the public site rendering the draft, in a new tab), Publish (copy the draft to the published website) and Discard changes (throw the draft away). Every save in the Website area writes the draft, so the bar is how the office knows what visitors can and cannot see yet.

  • Use it once per Website screen, above the content and the page editor.
  • Do not use it for things that have no draft: pages save at once, the board list saves at once, and documents keep their own Draft and Published states. On those screens the bar still shows, because the site content draft is shared.
  • Publish and Discard open a confirmation dialog; the outcome is announced with an alert in a live region.

Anatomy

  1. IconA warning triangle when there are changes, a check in a circle when everything is published.
  2. SentenceOne plain sentence in .publish-bar__text; it is the bar's accessible status.
  3. ActionsPreview site (a link to a new tab), Publish (primary) and Discard changes (ghost). Publish and Discard are disabled when there is no draft.

Variants

Two states of one bar, by the draft. Published: neutral, Publish and Discard disabled. Unpublished changes (.publish-bar--changes): the warning background, every action enabled. This second drawing is the unpublished-changes banner the Website area always shows.

Responsive behaviour

  • The bar is a wrapping flex row: the sentence takes at least 16rem and the actions wrap under it at 320px, each button on its own line when needed. No action is hidden behind a menu.
  • The bar is not sticky, so it never covers the editor's panes on a short window.

Theming

The published state uses --color-bg-subtle and --color-border-default; the changes state uses --color-warning-bg for the fill and --color-warning-fg for the border and the sentence. Both are defined in both themes and are checked below.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The bar is a role="status" region, so a change from "published" to "unpublished changes" after a save is announced politely. The three actions are a plain link and two buttons; Publish and Discard open an alertdialog that names the outcome ("Publish your changes?").

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabPreview site, Publish, Discard changes, in that order; disabled buttons are skipped.
Enter / SpaceOpens the preview tab or the confirmation dialog.

Focus

The shared focus ring on each control. After Publish or Discard completes, focus returns to the button that opened the dialog.

Labelling

"Preview site" says "(opens in a new tab)" in visually hidden text. The sentence is plain text; no icon-only control exists in the bar.

Content

  • Two sentences only: "Everything is published. Visitors see what you see." and "You have unpublished changes. Visitors still see the published website."
  • Actions are verbs: "Preview site", "Publish", "Discard changes". Never "Save", "Go live" or "Deploy".
  • No counts of changes: the sentence is true or false, and counting edits would mislead.
