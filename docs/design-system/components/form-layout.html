Form layout

The parts that hold an admin form together: one column of fields, the row of actions at the end, the error summary at the top, and what everything does while the form saves.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <form>

Overview

Form layout is a few small classes and one rule for the saving state. Field order, validation timing and how forms sit in pages and dialogs are in the forms pattern.

  • form.stack spaces the form fields evenly in one column (--space-4 apart).
  • .form-actions ends a form on a page: an end-aligned, wrapping row with the primary button last. In a dialog the same buttons sit in .dialog__footer.
  • .form-summary is a danger alert at the top of a form that could not be sent. It counts the problems and links each one to its field.
  • .grid with .span-7 and .span-5 puts a form beside its preview, as on the Theme tab of Settings.
  • Saving: the pressed button gets aria-busy="true", text fields become readonly, and selects, checkboxes and the other buttons become disabled.

The admin's forms: Sign in, Create account, Forgot password and Change password on their own pages; Add New User, Edit User, Create Invitation, New Maintenance Request and New Document in dialogs; and the theme form in Settings. .danger-zone also exists, for a destructive action that belongs to a whole record; no admin screen uses it, because deleting starts from a row action and a confirmation dialog.

Anatomy

  1. Error summary.alert.alert--danger.form-summary, role="alert", tabindex="-1"; an <ol> of links, one per problem, indented by --space-5.
  2. FieldsOne column of .fields, --space-4 apart from .stack. Each error sits under its field.
  3. Options rowA wrapping .cluster.cluster--between: "Remember me" at the start, "Forgot password?" at the end.
  4. Primary actionOn sign-in pages, one full-width .btn--primary.btn--block. Elsewhere, a .form-actions row.

Sizes

Form layout has no sizes; buttons and fields inside it carry their own.

Both themes

One thing to fix

  • Invalid: the browser bubbles are off (novalidate). On submit the form checks itself; the summary appears at the top and takes focus, and each field shows its own error. What was typed stays.
  • Saving: the pressed button is disabled aria-busy="true" and keeps its label; the spinner replaces the text visually. Text fields and textareas become readonly; selects, checkboxes, Cancel and the dialog's close button become disabled. Nothing moves, so there is no layout shift.
  • Failed (the server said no, or could not be reached) is not a summary: it is a danger alert with role="alert" above the fields, and the form returns to its default state with the values kept. New Document's "published, but not sent to members" message is this state.

Responsive behaviour

  • Forms are one column at every width, so they reflow at 320px and at 400% zoom without sideways scrolling. Fields are full width; short ones such as Scope may be capped with an inline max-width.
  • .form-actions wraps. At 320px Cancel and Create user still share a line; if they ever do not fit, the primary wraps below and stays at the end.
  • The options row on Sign in is a wrapping .cluster; "Forgot password?" moves under the checkbox when space runs out.
  • .span-7 and .span-5 are full width until 1024px, so the theme preview sits under its form on phones and tablets.
  • The error summary is always the first thing in the form, so it is in view when focus moves to it on any screen.

Theming

The summary uses the danger alert's tokens (--color-danger-bg, --color-danger-fg, --color-danger-icon, --color-danger-border) with a --space-1 wide left edge. Everything else comes from the fields and buttons inside, so a form follows both themes with no overrides.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native <form> named by its heading (aria-labelledby) with novalidate, so the system's own messages replace the browser bubbles. The summary is role="alert" with tabindex="-1": it is announced and receives focus after a failed submit (WCAG 3.3.1, 3.3.3). A server failure is a separate role="alert" message.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter in a text fieldSubmits the form. This only works when the fields and the submit button are inside one <form>.
Tab after a failed submitFocus is on the summary; Tab reaches its first link.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field, scrolled into view.
Esc in a dialog formCloses the dialog, as Cancel does. Not while it is saving.

Focus

In a dialog, focus starts on the first field. After a failed submit it moves to the summary. While saving it stays put: read-only fields keep it, and when the request ends focus goes to the result (the toast, the summary, the failure alert or the next page).

Labelling

Every field has a visible label tied with for. Help that applies to one field, such as "At least 8 characters", sits under that field and joins it with aria-describedby, not in a paragraph above the form. The summary title counts the problems; each link names the problem. A busy button keeps its name and adds aria-busy="true".

Motion

None of its own. The summary appears without animation; the busy button's spinner follows the button rules and stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Summary links are text links in a list whose line height gives each row at least 24px. Buttons are 44px tall; the sign-in primary spans the form.

Content

  • Summary titles are short and calm: "One thing to fix", "Two things to fix".
  • Summary links name the problem ("Enter your username"); the field error says how to fix it.
  • A failure says what happened and what to do: "We couldn't sign you in. Check your username and password, then try again."
  • Primary labels say what saving does, in sentence case: "Create user", "Save theme", "Sign in". Not "Submit".
  • Labels and headings are sentence case: "Confirm new password", not "Confirm New Password".
  • No placeholders as hints; put a hint under the field where it stays visible.
  • While saving, the button keeps its words. Do not change them to "Signing in...".

Drift from the implemented admin

  • Spacing and actions. The admin spaces fields with .form-group (16px bottom margin) and ends forms with .modal-actions in dialogs, .btn-row on Change password and .theme-actions on Settings. The system uses form.stack, .form-actions on pages and .dialog__footer in dialogs.
  • No error summary. No admin form has one. Sign in, Create account and Change password show .error-message text under each field, not joined with aria-describedby and with no aria-invalid; only text and password inputs get the red .invalid border.
  • Errors without words. New Maintenance Request and Add New User show touched fields with a red border only (.ng-invalid.ng-touched), with no message.
  • Failure alert. Sign in and Create account show .alert.alert-danger ("Invalid username or password") above the form without role="alert", so it is not announced. New Document's .notice-alert--danger does use role="alert", which is the system's rule.
  • Saving. Sign in and Create account change the button to "Signing in..." and "Creating...", and leave every field and "Remember me" editable. New Document disables Cancel, Close and the checkbox but leaves Title, Type, Content and Status editable. The system keeps the label, adds aria-busy, makes text fields read-only and disables the rest.
  • Form boundaries. New Document's fields and buttons sit in a div, not a <form>, and its buttons have no type. On Settings, Scope and the Save Theme button sit outside the form, and "Reset to Default" is an <a> with no href, which a keyboard cannot reach.
  • Labels and hints. Dialog labels have no for and repeat their name in aria-label. Auth pages use placeholders such as "Enter your username" as hints. Change password puts "Password must be at least 8 characters long" in a paragraph above the form. Labels and buttons are title case ("Confirm New Password", "Save Theme").
  • Look. Green 8px-radius buttons (.btn-primary, .btn-secondary, .btn-block) and Outfit text; the system uses violet pill buttons, rounded system fonts and both themes.
