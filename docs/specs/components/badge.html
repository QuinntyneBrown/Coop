Sizes

One size: --text-caption at medium weight with --space-0-5 by --space-3 padding. The design-system page says not to add sizes; width comes from the word.

New Completed Terminée
Short, long and French labels at the one size. AC-7, AC-19

States

The badge is read-only: no hover, focus, active, disabled or busy state. The only change is replacement by the next word and tone, shown here as each tone's English and French word.
