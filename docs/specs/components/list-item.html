List item

Sizes

One size. The leading visual is 2.25rem (avatar) or 3rem (icon tile); rows are at least 60 px tall with a visual and 45 px without.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state; selected and unread use the real attributes and markup.

Responsive

The row has no media rules; it is fluid. The frames show the avatar row with its badge and a long asset name wrapping at 320 px, the dark rendering at 360 px and the notice rows at 768 px.
