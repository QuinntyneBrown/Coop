Variants

The seven glyphs the product uses, each a Material Icons ligature with an explicit aria-label (D-5). Bell, menu, edit, delete, close, back, upload. AC-1, AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, AC-12, AC-19
The bell with no count, with two unread, and with 120 unread ("99+"). The badge is aria-hidden; the name carries "2 unread" (D-7, D-8). AC-6, AC-7, AC-8, AC-9
Menu button with the drawer closed and open (aria-expanded, same name, no fill of its own, D-9, D-10), and a toggle with aria-pressed for the contract (D-12). AC-10, AC-11, AC-20

Both themes

The product ships one light appearance; the dark block below is required by the CRD format and renders the same tokens (D-2).

Responsive

The icon button has no media rules; the frames show the public top bar, a users table row, a viewer header and a chat header at 320, 360 and 768 px. The hamburger is hidden at 1024 px and wider by the top bar, not by this component.
