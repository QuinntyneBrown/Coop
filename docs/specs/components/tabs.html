Settings: three tabs with id="settings", "Theme" selected by default, each tab carrying its data-testid. The mocks' bordered .tab look is replaced (D-4). AC-4, AC-5, AC-11, AC-19

Sizes

One size: a 36 px tab from the --text-body-sm line plus --space-2 padding; width comes from the label.

States

Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state; selection uses the real aria-selected. A selected tab does not change on hover.

Responsive
