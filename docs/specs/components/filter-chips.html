Filter chips

Single-select pill toggles that narrow the maintenance and document lists; one pressed chip, a labelled group, a wrapping row. Requirements: filter-chips.md. Design system: filter-chips.

Variants

Admin documents: four chips in a labelled group, "All Documents" pressed, test ids forwarded to the buttons. AC-1, AC-2, AC-5
Admin maintenance: the five L2-REQ-025 statuses with "New" pressed. AC-3, AC-7
Public rows render the same .filter-btn pill (D-4); "All" is pressed when value is null. Labels are the page's (D-13). AC-4, AC-10

Sizes

One size: a 38 px chip from the --text-body-sm line, --space-2 padding and hairline edges; width comes from the label.

States

Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state; pressed uses the real aria-pressed. A pressed chip does not change on hover, and no disabled or loading chip exists (D-9).

Responsive

The chips have no media rules; the group wraps (D-6, applied here as a specimen style until components.css catches up). Frames show the admin, public and French rows at 320, 360 and 768 px.
