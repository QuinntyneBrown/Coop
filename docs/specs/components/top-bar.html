Top bar

Three bars from one markup: the member app bar, the admin page bar with its heading and search, and the marketing bar; the bell with and without a count; the hamburger collapsed and expanded. Requirements: top-bar.md. Design system: top bar.

Sizes

One size: 4rem tall (the 2.5rem icon button plus two --space-3) and the viewport width, with the inner row capped at --layout-container-max.

States

The bar has no states of its own. Hover, focus and active on its icon buttons are forced with data-state; the count and aria-expanded are real attributes.

Responsive

Each frame is a real viewport with the sticky bar above page content. The rules that hide the hamburger from 1024 px, hide the projected actions below it when a hamburger exists, and wrap the inner row are specimen CSS marked in the frame source (D-5, D-8).
