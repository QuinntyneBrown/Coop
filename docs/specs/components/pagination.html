Sizes

One size: buttons at least 2rem square with a --text-body-sm label; the container fills the table card.

States

Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state; current and disabled use the real attributes. The current page does not change on hover and never disables.

Responsive

No media rule targets the pagination; the container wraps (D-8, applied here as a specimen style until components.css catches up). Frames show the table card at 320 and 360 px (controls under the summary) and 768 px (one row).
