Sizes

One size: inputs and selects are 2.5rem tall, a textarea 6.25rem and resizable vertically; width is always 100 % of the group. AC-2

States

Focus is forced with data-state; invalid, disabled and read-only use the real attributes. The host tag coop-form-field carries .form-group (D-4).

Responsive

The field itself has no media rule. The frames show .form-row stacked at 320 and 360 px as required (D-8) beside the current side-by-side drift, a wrapping error, and the row at 768 px.
